Helping Every Student Walk In Prepared

SupplySpark is a student-led nonprofit that provides school supplies to foster children in need, helping every student feel prepared, confident, and ready to succeed.

Get Involved

Our Mission

Our mission is to support foster children by providing essential school supplies that help them succeed academically and feel confident in the classroom.


We aim to create equal opportunities for foster students by ensuring every child has access to the tools they need to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.

Our Process

1. Community Donations

We collect school supplies and financial donations from individuals, businesses, and local organizations.

2. Organizing Supplies

Volunteers sort, package, and prepare supplies for distribution.

3. Partnering With Organizations

We work with foster agencies, schools, shelters, and community groups to identify students in need.

4. Delivering Supplies

Supplies are distributed directly to foster children before and during the school year.

Our Impact

Although SupplySpark is just getting started, our goal is to make a lasting difference in the lives of foster children through education support and community involvement.

  • Donating hundreds of supplies each year
  • Expanding partnerships with local schools and foster organizations
  • Hosting annual school supply drives
  • Building long-term support programs for students

About the Founders

SupplySpark was started by Byron Chen and Owen Huang to help foster children gain access to the school supplies they need to succeed. Their goal is to build a strong community effort that gives foster children the tools and encouragement they deserve.

FAQ

Who does SupplySpark help?

SupplySpark supports foster children and students in need by providing school supplies.


How can I donate?

You can donate school supplies, make a financial contribution, or volunteer your time.


Are donated supplies required to be new?

No. New or lightly used items in good condition are welcome.
